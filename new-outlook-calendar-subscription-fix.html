New Outlook Won't Add Your Web Calendar? Here's the Fix

The Kernel Patch — plain-language tech notes

You try to subscribe to a web calendar — a club calendar, a school calendar, a speaker series — and New Outlook throws a vague "Sorry, we can't do that right now" and gives up. It's not you, and it's not the calendar's fault.

What's actually going on

New Outlook (and modern versions of desktop Outlook) use a newer authentication system, and they announce that capability to whatever server is hosting the calendar — even when the calendar doesn't require a login at all. Some calendar servers don't know what to do with that announcement and simply refuse the connection instead of just handing over the calendar. It's a compatibility hiccup, not something wrong with your account.

The fix that usually works

Add the subscription through Outlook on the web instead of the desktop app — the browser version doesn't run into the same handshake problem, and once it's added there, it shows up automatically in New Outlook too.

  1. Go to outlook.office.com (or outlook.com) and sign in.
  2. Open Calendar in the left-hand menu.
  3. Select Add calendar → From internet.
  4. Paste in the calendar's link (it should end in .ics; if you only have a "webcal://" link, that usually works too).
  5. Give it a name and color, then select Save.

Give it a few minutes, then check New Outlook or your desktop app — the calendar should now appear alongside your others.

Where to find the calendar's link: look for a "Subscribe," "iCal," or "Add to calendar" button on the organization's website — it's usually a small link icon near their event listings.
