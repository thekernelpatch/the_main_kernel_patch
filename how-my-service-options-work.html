How My Service Options Work

The Kernel Patch — plain-language tech notes

Not every tech problem fits the same box, so I offer a few different ways to work together. Here's the plain-English breakdown.

Project-based

A specific piece of work with a clear start and finish — setting up a new computer, migrating your email, cleaning up a messy inbox, fixing a recurring issue. We scope it out, I give you a quote, and we're done when it's done.

Service-based

Ongoing, defined support for something that needs regular attention — keeping your systems patched and backed up, managing your devices, or handling recurring maintenance. This is more of a standing relationship than a one-off fix.

Hourly / on-call

For the "I don't need much, but I want someone to call when something breaks" situation. You get a set number of hours a month (or pay as you go), and use them for whatever comes up — quick questions, small fixes, or just talking through a decision before you make it.

Not sure which one fits?

Most people don't know which category they need until we talk it through — that's normal, and it's exactly what the first conversation is for.
